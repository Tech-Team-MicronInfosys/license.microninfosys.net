SECURITY POLICY NOTICE

Purpose:
This standard defines baseline security reporting and response expectations for
Micron Infosys repositories and related project assets.

1. Scope
Applies to public and private Micron Infosys codebases, including client and
in-house projects where security disclosures are permitted by contract.

2. How To Report A Vulnerability
Do not open public issues for suspected vulnerabilities.
Report security concerns privately to the project security contact listed in the
repository profile, organization contact page, or contractual support channel.
Include:
- affected project/repository,
- affected versions or commit references,
- reproduction steps or proof of concept,
- impact summary and severity estimate.

3. Coordinated Disclosure
Micron Infosys will review reports and coordinate remediation and disclosure in
a responsible manner, prioritizing user safety, client obligations, and legal
constraints.

4. Response Expectations
Target response windows (best effort):
- acknowledgement: within 5 business days;
- triage decision: within 10 business days;
- remediation timeline: based on severity and operational impact.

5. Safe Harbor
Micron Infosys does not pursue action for good-faith security research that:
- avoids privacy violations and service disruption,
- avoids data destruction or unauthorized data extraction,
- is reported privately and responsibly.

6. Out Of Scope
The following are generally out of scope unless they create material security
risk:
- low-impact informational findings,
- social engineering attempts against staff,
- denial-of-service testing without prior written approval.

7. Priority Of Signed Agreements
If a signed client agreement or legal requirement specifies different security
reporting terms, that signed agreement controls for that project.

8. Public Citation Use
This notice may be cited as the baseline security standard for Micron Infosys
projects and repositories.
